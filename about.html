About Meridian Solutions

Personalized learning begins with the individual—their interests, strengths, needs, and goals.

Our Mission

Meridian Solutions creates personalized learning experiences that support the development of practical life skills, confidence, communication, and greater independence through music, media, technology, and structured direct instruction.

Our Philosophy

Planning, organization, problem-solving, communication, emotional regulation, responsibility, and self-expression can develop naturally through meaningful creative work and structured, direct learning experiences.

Why Music, Media, and Technology

Meridian’s instructional approach is informed by research supporting the use of music-based learning experiences to facilitate life-skill development. The learning environments offer real reasons to prepare, make decisions, solve problems, communicate, accept feedback, and complete projects. They also allow learning to begin with interests that may already be important to the individual.

Our Learning Environments

Meridian Solutions provides multiple learning environments through which individuals can develop practical life skills, confidence, and greater independence. Each environment offers a different way to engage in learning while sessions remain personalized around the individual’s strengths, interests, goals, and preferred learning style.

Audio Production

Audio production provides a hands-on environment for developing patience, attention to detail, problem-solving, and the ability to follow a process from beginning to end. As individuals record, edit, arrange, and refine audio projects, they practice planning, sequencing, decision-making, accepting feedback, and making thoughtful revisions. Projects are adapted to the individual’s interests and experience level while supporting skills that can transfer to school, work, creative projects, and everyday responsibilities.

DJ Performance & Curation

DJ performance and curation provide an engaging environment for developing confidence, adaptability, preparation, and awareness of others. Selecting and organizing music strengthens categorization, planning, and decision-making, while practicing transitions encourages concentration, timing, and persistence. Individuals may also develop communication and social awareness by considering an audience, responding to feedback, and learning how to adjust when circumstances change.

Media Production & Content Strategies

Media production and content strategies help individuals turn ideas into organized, purposeful projects. Planning, recording, editing, and sharing content provide opportunities to strengthen communication, creativity, time management, organization, and follow-through. Individuals learn to consider their audience, make responsible content decisions, respond constructively to feedback, and develop repeatable processes for completing projects. These skills can support personal expression, academic work, entrepreneurship, and future employment.

Digital Literacy & Online Safety

Digital literacy and online safety help individuals use technology with greater confidence, responsibility, and independence. Sessions may address evaluating information, protecting personal information, managing accounts, recognizing online risks, communicating appropriately, and making thoughtful decisions in digital spaces. Instruction is connected to the individual’s actual technology use so that safe and responsible habits can be practiced in realistic situations and applied outside of sessions.

Life Skills Essentials

Life Skills Essentials provides a direct and structured pathway for individuals who benefit from explicit instruction, guided practice, and clearly defined steps. Sessions may address communication, organization, decision-making, self-advocacy, routines, goal setting, and other skills connected to the individual’s daily life. Activities and supports are selected according to the individual’s strengths, needs, and learning plan, with the goal of helping skills become practical, repeatable, and increasingly independent.

A Personalized Approach

Every individual begins in a different place. Sessions are shaped around current abilities, preferred interests, support needs, and meaningful goals.

Meaningful Progress

Progress may look different for every person. It can include working independently for longer periods, communicating more clearly, managing frustration, organizing a project, following through on a task, or using a new skill in everyday life.

How Learning Takes Place

Instruction is provided through live, one-to-one online sessions, allowing each learning experience to be personalized to the individual’s strengths, interests, goals, and pace of learning. Flexible scheduling makes it easier to fit sessions around school, work, and other commitments.

Who We Serve

Meridian Solutions serves adolescents and adults seeking to develop practical life skills through personalized instruction. Individuals may come with a wide range of interests, goals, learning styles, and support needs.
